THE EDITING REALITY

One crafting animation takes 15 to 30 minutes by hand.

Slicing inventory grids, setting anchors by eye, and pasting the same easing curves across dozens of tracks eats hours of every upload. AgentMotion replaces manual setup with editable timelines.

BY HAND IN PREMIERE & AFTER EFFECTS

  • "Swap Netherite for Diamond" - on a flat MP4 pack or template, you have to re-render the entire file from scratch.
  • Blurry, mushy pixel art: Premiere Pro's default bilinear sampling smooths and blurs sharp pixel art during scaling.
  • Tedious keyframe pasting: manually copying Bezier ease handles across 4 to 10 item layers for every single recipe.
  • Fix one easing curve? Do it again on every layer: adjusting timing requires manually shifting keyframes across 10+ clips.

WITH AGENTMOTION

  • Every layer isolated on its own track: re-time, swap an icon, hide a layer, or tweak opacity directly in Effect Controls.
  • Pixel-sharp at integer scales: whole-number nearest-neighbor scaling (100%, 200%, 300%) preserves clean pixel edges with zero blur.
  • Native Premiere keyframes: clean Bezier curves on Position, Scale, and Opacity that you can grab, tweak, and retime.
  • ~21s automated pipeline: the extension renders all layers in 19 seconds and inserts the sequence into Premiere in 1.7 seconds.

HOW IT WORKS // 3 SIMPLE STEPS

From prompt to timeline in three steps.

You describe the scene. AgentMotion renders every element as a sharp transparent layer. The Premiere Pro panel places them on your timeline, already animated.

  1. STEP 01 - SCENE DEFINITION

    Describe or Pick a Recipe

    Type what you need in plain English (e.g. "diamond pickaxe crafting with ease-in entrance") or select from the recipe library. Slot coordinates and item timings are calculated automatically.

  2. STEP 02 - LAYER RENDERING

    Layers Render Sharp

    Every item, slot, button, and letter becomes its own transparent PNG, scaled in whole numbers so pixels stay sharp. Reproducible pixel rendering for every scene.

  3. STEP 03 - 1.7s TIMELINE INSERT

    Instant Premiere Pro Timeline

    Click insert. The panel creates a 60fps sequence, places each layer on its own video track (V1–V19), and writes native Bezier keyframes for Position, Scale, and Opacity.
